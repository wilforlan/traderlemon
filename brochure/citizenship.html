Citizenship

A person's guide to the street, the bank, and the stall. Read this before you stamp your papers.

The first economy pays rent and wages. Citizenship starts at the door of this public scene. You are given pocket money. You can leave with a measure that is still yours at the bank and at a friend's table.

The visa

credentials.json is your visa

The immigration desk gives you one file. That file is how you come back. It is also how someone else could enter as you.

Keep it private. Do not share it with anyone.

Anyone who holds the file can act as your node. v0peer does not store a copy and cannot reset a lost file. Do not paste it into chat, email, a support ticket, or a shared drive. Store it offline, or in a password manager only you can open. Upload it only at a desk you opened yourself.

  1. Stand at the World 1 or World 2 immigration desk.
  2. Issue new papers, or restore the file you already saved.
  3. Download credentials.json before you walk onto the street.
  4. Check that you can open the file later, on a device you control.
  5. Bring the same file when you return to the bank or the stall.

A request for a seed phrase is outside this economy.

Standing nearby, unread and unpaid, mints nothing. A charge you cannot cover is refused whole. Nobody is credited for the half you could not pay.

APW$ is pocket money on the grounds. It does not withdraw as rent.

What a citizen can do

Eight moves, in the order a night uses them

  1. Enter on one passport. Friends see the same shelf you see.
  2. Play. A finished round can pay APU, at least 5 on the first round, inside 100 for the UTC day. Play spends no APW$.
  3. Buy. The price leaves in APW$. You receive 3 APU per whole dollar. A $4.99 item pays 12 APU. The shelf shows the item sold.
  4. Talk with an agent at $1.50 a minute. You can receive 1 APU per 10 billed seconds, and at most 5 in the sitting.
  5. Reach another person who is in reach. You pay. They receive world dollars. No APU is minted.
  6. Invite a real account for 25 APU, inside 1,000 in the month. Give at least 10 APU. Smaller gifts are refused whole.
  7. Lock Flex, Growth, or Anchor. Interest posts when the term completes. Leave in the first 48 hours and you receive the principal.
  8. Release APU from the world purse into the bankable balance, then use Econext or the stall.
A night you can check

Start at $10 and 0 APU. One billed minute with an agent costs $1.50 and pays 5 APU. A $4.99 purchase pays 12 APU. You hold $3.51 and 17 APU. Those 17 are real. Cash-out starts at 50 APU and about $50 of world dollars.

The purse on your screen is your purse. Accepting a call does not copy anyone else's wallet onto it.

Charges

A tax on the transaction, taken when it happens

25 percent

Human message or voice. You pay the whole price in APW$. The office keeps a quarter. The other person keeps the rest. The dollars already existed. No new money is printed.

12.5 percent

A settled stall ticket. The buyer pays the quoted SOL. The seller receives 87.5 percent. The treasury receives 12.5 percent, in SOL, in the same office that pays cash-out.

  • Holdings are left alone. Unbilled time is left alone.
  • A refused message, a missed ring, and a one-legged stall ticket charge nobody.
  • SOL you already hold outside this scene is your own affair. This office does not add a tax to it.
  • Cash-out also subtracts a small disclosed network fee. In the town that fee is 0.000005 SOL. It is a third number, beside the 25 percent and the 12.5 percent.

The charge is the office's share of a deal that happened.

Econext and the stall

Two ways out. They do not share a price.

The bank
  1. Release APU so it is bankable.
  2. Hold at least 50 APU.
  3. Show about $50 of world dollars.
  4. Accept the quote: 0.00029359 SOL per APU.
  5. Wait 7 to 14 business days. The day's payouts stay within 10 SOL.
  6. SOL arrives at the address you connected.
The stall
  1. Post an amount of APU and a SOL quote.
  2. Those APU sit in escrow until the ask ends.
  3. A buyer pays the quote.
  4. You receive 87.5 percent. The treasury receives 12.5 percent. The buyer receives the APU.
  5. If either leg is missing, every change is zero.
  6. Cancel, and the APU returns to you.

Jules, in the town, holds $10, so the bank stays dark and he uses the stall. Rex burns 500 APU for $50, then converts 50 APU at the book rate. Both are citizenship. The word on the week — stress, hold, or expand — describes the window. It does not change 0.00029359.
